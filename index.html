Privacy policy

Updated: October 2, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy applies to the app (Husk), intended for adults aged 18 and over worldwide. No account is required. The provider’s support email and online policy are listed below; development and acceptance builds may not have these addresses configured.

Video and audio

Conversion, trimming and playback happen on your device. The app does not send source videos, exported audio, filenames, paths or media content to the developer or advertising services. It accesses files you select or authorize through the system. Exports are saved in the public music folder shown in the app. When you choose to share or open files elsewhere, the receiving app applies its own policy. Cloud file providers may download or synchronize files themselves.

Speech to text

Transcription runs on your device; audio and transcripts are never uploaded. The first time you use a quality level, the app downloads that speech model (about 31 MB or 60 MB) once from Hugging Face, a third-party model host. Like any download, the request shows that host your IP address and standard connection details; the app sends no media, text or app identifiers with it, and Hugging Face’s privacy policy applies to the request: https://huggingface.co/privacy . Speech recognition can make mistakes, so check important details before relying on the text.

Local storage and deletion

A private database stores filenames, URIs, conversion settings and results. Temporary audio supports conversion and transcription. Transcripts and downloaded speech models are also kept in private app storage; you can delete transcripts in the app and models in Settings. Settings, cached purchase entitlement, pending acknowledgments and usage counts stay locally. Private app data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private records; exported audio in public storage may remain and can be deleted in the library or a file manager. Other apps, user-initiated synchronization and separate manufacturer backup services are outside the app’s control.

Advertising and choices

The free version uses Google AdMob. Its SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can estimate approximate location), interactions such as launches and ad taps, diagnostics and performance information, advertising IDs, app set IDs and applicable device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and security. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Offline conversion does not depend on ad connectivity. Keeping media on-device does not mean advertising and store services never connect to the internet.

Google UMP manages applicable consent and opt-out choices, including messages for the EEA, UK, Switzerland and applicable US states. Privacy options remain available in Settings whenever UMP requires them, including after purchasing ad removal. You can change choices there and reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Declining personalized ads does not necessarily stop all ads or SDK data processing. Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

Purchases and reviews

If the app offers the optional one-time ad removal purchase, Google Play processes it. The app reads order and acknowledgment status to deliver and restore access, and does not receive card details. After purchase, the app stops requesting and displaying ads; Google’s policies govern previously processed data and retention. Google Play provides in-app reviews and handles reviews and related information. This version does not include Firebase Crashlytics or additional business analytics SDKs.

Voluntary support and diagnostics

Only when you choose to send does your mail app send a diagnostic email. It includes audio codec, container, duration, error code, device model, Android version and app version, without media, filenames or paths. You can edit it before sending. Your email address, message and anything you add reach the support provider to answer you and investigate issues. They are retained only as needed for support and necessary records; contact support to request access, correction or deletion. Your email provider processes messages under its policy.

Your rights and changes

You can delete local records and audio, manage applicable consent or opt-out rights through Privacy options, or contact support about data requests. Requests about Google’s data can also be made through its privacy pages. Depending on your location, rights may include access, deletion, correction, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent and complaints to a regulator. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Policy changes ship with app updates and are reflected in the online policy.
